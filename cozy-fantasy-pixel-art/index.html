FAQ

Do the Frostbrook DLCs need the base pack?

No. The Christmas DLC and the Town Interiors DLC work on their own, but they share the palette and grid of Frostbrook Snow Village so everything fits together.

Which engines are supported?

Tilesets include Tiled, Godot 4 and RPG Maker MZ files; sprites and VFX are PNG sheets that work in Unity, GameMaker and any 2D engine.

Is there a free sample?

Yes: Frostbrook, Elemental Spells and Dungeon Warriors each have a free sample.

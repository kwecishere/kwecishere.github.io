FAQ

Are the animals and vehicles animated?

The farm animals, reindeer and huskies have baked animations (walk, run, idle and more). Cars and the pickup have separate wheel nodes and a drivable Godot 4 demo.

Which formats are included?

GLB (glTF 2.0) and OBJ + MTL for every model, with small PNG textures. They import into Godot 4, Unity, Unreal and Blender.

Can I try them first?

Yes, there are free samples for the cars, farm animals, orchard and Christmas village.

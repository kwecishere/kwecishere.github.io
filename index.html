PSX Horror Asset Packs (LO-FI DREAD)

LO-FI DREAD is a series of PS1 / PSX-style horror asset packs. Every pack uses the same real-world scale, a small 15-bit texture atlas and the same PSX shader (vertex snapping and affine texture warping), so you can mix an abandoned hospital with a haunted forest or a dead shopping mall in one game. Most packs ship as a ready Godot 4 project with a walkable demo level; the GLB and OBJ files also work in Unity, Unreal Engine and Blender.

Modern Town – Top-Down 16x16 Tilesets & Characters

Modern Town is a set of top-down (3/4 view) pixel art tilesets drawn on the same 16 px grid with the same outline, palette and light direction. Every pack comes in 16, 32 and 48 px, with and without baked shadows, and ships Tiled (.tsx/.tmx) files, Godot 4 TileSets, RPG Maker MZ sheets and single PNG sprites for Unity and GameMaker. The animated 16x32 characters (town NPCs and a sci-fi crew) use the same style.

PSX Horror Asset Packs (LO-FI DREAD)

LO-FI DREAD is a series of PS1 / PSX-style horror asset packs. Every pack uses the same real-world scale, a small 15-bit texture atlas and the same PSX shader (vertex snapping and affine texture warping), so you can mix an abandoned hospital with a haunted forest or a dead shopping mall in one game. Most packs ship as a ready Godot 4 project with a walkable demo level; the GLB and OBJ files also work in Unity, Unreal Engine and Blender.

FAQ

Which engines do the PSX horror packs support?

Godot 4 gets a ready project with demo levels and the PSX shader. The GLB and OBJ models import into Unity, Unreal Engine 5 and Blender; textures are plain PNG atlases.

Do the packs share the same scale and style?

Yes. All LO-FI DREAD packs use real-world metres, a 2 m modular grid and the same PS1 shader, so pieces from different packs fit together.

Is there a free version?

Yes, the LO-FI DREAD Free Sampler has 15 models, textures and the PSX shader in a small Godot 4 gallery.

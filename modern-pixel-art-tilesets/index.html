FAQ

What tile size are the Modern Town tilesets?

They are drawn at 16x16 and exported at 16, 32 and 48 px with nearest-neighbour scaling, so they stay crisp. Use the 48 px sheets for RPG Maker MZ.

Do they work in Godot 4 and Tiled?

Yes. Each pack has Godot 4 .tres TileSets, Tiled .tsx tilesets and sample .tmx maps, plus single PNG sprites for any other engine.

Are there characters that match?

Yes: Modern Town Characters (41 animated NPCs) and Space Crew & Aliens (54 sci-fi characters) use the same scale, outline and shadow.
